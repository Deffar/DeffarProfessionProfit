# Deffar Profession Profit

Find out which profession crafts are actually worth your time. Open it with `/dpp`.

A World of Warcraft: Forever addon.

## What it does
- **Gold per hour:** every learned recipe (Cooking, Engineering, Tailoring) with its material cost, sale value, profit per craft and profit per hour, using Auctionator and vendor prices.
- **Enchanting:** ranks disenchants by what they are worth against what the item costs at the auction house.
- **Luck tracker:** compares your extra results (like Master Chef procs) with what you should expect, per session, character or lifetime.
- **AH sales:** shows what your auctions really brought in, with deposits and the auction house cut included.
- **Vendor + button:** buys the materials your recipes need from the vendor you are visiting, based on what you already hold.
- **Create All + button:** on your profession window, starts Create All on the most profitable craft you can make from your bags.
- **Auctionator shopping lists:** one click on a recipe builds a list with price limits that keep the craft profitable.

## Notes
- Open each profession window once so your recipes are saved, and visit vendors so their prices are saved.
- Auctionator is optional but gives the most useful prices.
- `/dpp` opens the window. `/dpp status` shows what was read and saved.

## Install
Download `DeffarProfessionProfit.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/Deffar/DeffarProfessionProfit/releases/latest) and extract it into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`, so that the folder `AddOns\DeffarProfessionProfit` contains the `.toc` file. (GitHub's green "Code > Download ZIP" button gives a folder named `...-main` with the files directly inside; use the release zip instead.)
