# Deffar Profession Profit

Find out which profession crafts are actually worth your time. Open it with `/dpp`.

A World of Warcraft: Forever addon.

## What it does
- **Gold per hour:** every learned recipe (Cooking, Engineering, Tailoring, Leatherworking, Alchemy, Blacksmithing, First Aid, Mining) with its material cost, sale value, profit per craft and profit per hour, using Auctionator and vendor prices.
- **Enchanting:** ranks disenchants by what they are worth against what the item costs at the auction house.
- **Luck tracker:** compares your extra results (like Master Chef procs) with what you should expect, per session, character or lifetime.
- **AH sales:** shows what your auctions really brought in, with deposits and the auction house cut included.
- **Vendor + button:** buys the materials your recipes need from the vendor you are visiting, based on what you already hold. Hold Shift to buy for the crafts that earn most at the auction house.
- **Create All + button:** on your profession window, starts Create All on the most profitable craft you can make from your bags. When recipes share a material it lets you pick one.
- **Nearest vendor:** hover a material to see the closest vendor that sells it; Ctrl-click pins it on the map.
- **Known recipes:** untick the box on a profession tab to see every obtainable recipe you have not learned, with where it comes from and its zone, blueprints included. Ctrl-click one to pin the vendor, the quest giver, the creature it drops from or the nearest trainer on the map.
- **Next rank:** the golden ? beside the X tells how to get your next profession rank (trainer, book or quest, where, and what it needs); click it to pin the place.
- **Cooking foods by buff:** the Effect box lists only foods that give Stamina, Strength, Agility and the rest, with a Stat column to sort by.
- **Minimap button:** left-click opens the window, right-click the luck window, drag to move it (`/dpp minimap on|off`).
- **Auctionator shopping lists:** one click on a recipe builds a list with price limits that keep the craft profitable.

## Notes
- Open each profession window once so your recipes are saved, and visit vendors so their prices are saved.
- Auctionator is optional but gives the most useful prices.
- `/dpp` opens the window. `/dpp status` shows what was read and saved.

## Install
Download `DeffarProfessionProfit.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/Deffar/DeffarProfessionProfit/releases/latest) and extract it into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`, so that the folder `AddOns\DeffarProfessionProfit` contains the `.toc` file. (GitHub's green "Code > Download ZIP" button gives a folder named `...-main` with the files directly inside; use the release zip instead.)
